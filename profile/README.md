# Starklips Otomotiv

Starklips Otomotiv is a Türkiye-based manufacturer specializing in automotive clips, fasteners, assembly products and related automotive solutions for OEM and aftermarket applications.

## Product Groups

- Automotive Clips
- Automotive Fasteners
- Window Regulator & Window Mechanism Parts
- Sunroof Parts
- Automotive Electrical Products
- Automotive Chemicals
- PDR Products
- Professional Repair & Service Products

## OEM & Aftermarket Solutions

We manufacture and supply automotive fastening and assembly solutions for manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers and aftermarket companies.

Our product range includes plastic clips, metal fasteners, retainers, trim clips, panel clips, bumper clips and various automotive assembly components.

## Global Business

Starklips Otomotiv works with customers and business partners in Türkiye and international markets.

We are open to cooperation with:

- Automotive manufacturers
- OEM suppliers
- Tier suppliers
- Automotive aftermarket distributors
- Wholesalers

- Industrial suppliers
